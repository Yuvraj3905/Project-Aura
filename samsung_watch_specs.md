# Key Differences: Watch 8 vs Watch 7

- **Design**: Watch 8 adopted the cushion/squircle case; Watch 7 used the traditional round case.
- **Display brightness**: Watch 8 up to 3000 nits vs Watch 7 up to 2000 nits.
- **Classic model**: Watch 8 Classic (46mm) with rotating bezel exists; Watch 7 had no Classic (the Watch 6 Classic preceded it).
- **Health sensors**: Watch 8 added Antioxidant Index and Vascular Load; Watch 7 introduced the AGEs Index.
- **Software**: Watch 8 ships with Wear OS 6 / One UI 8 Watch + Gemini; Watch 7 shipped with Wear OS 5 / One UI 6 Watch.
